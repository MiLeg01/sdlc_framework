# Timeline pattern

- Best use: ordered phases with milestones, gates, owners, or dependencies.
- Avoid: unrelated categories, precise quantitative comparison, or a decorative line with no temporal meaning.
- Capacity: 3-7 phases; group dense milestones within phases instead of flattening every event.
- Interaction: optional phase focus, detail disclosure, or dependency highlighting.
- Responsive: stack phases vertically while preserving order and gate labels.
- Accessibility: encode status and sequence with text and structure, not color or position alone.
