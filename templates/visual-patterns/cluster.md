# Cluster pattern

- Best use: related capabilities, themes, systems, or findings grouped around a clear organizing principle.
- Avoid: implying hierarchy or causality when only similarity is known.
- Capacity: 3-6 groups with concise labels and explicit cross-group relationships when they matter.
- Interaction: optional group focus, filtering, or relationship reveal.
- Responsive: preserve group membership in stacked cards or labeled lists.
- Accessibility: provide a textual group summary and do not rely on spatial proximity alone.
