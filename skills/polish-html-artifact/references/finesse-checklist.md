# HTML visual-finesse checklist

Use the categories that apply to the artifact. Record unavailable states as limits, not passes.

## Invariants

- [ ] factual source, claims, and evidence are unchanged
- [ ] dashboard section IDs, order, navigation, data model, and detail interaction are unchanged
- [ ] presentation narrative, section order, technical depth, and call to action are unchanged
- [ ] client brand and locale policy remain authoritative

## Typography

- [ ] display, body, metadata, data, code, chart, and table roles are distinct
- [ ] heading levels have restrained but visible contrast
- [ ] paragraph and heading measures support one-pass reading
- [ ] weights, line heights, and letter spacing are deliberate
- [ ] technical identifiers and evidence paths remain readable and copyable

## Composition and hierarchy

- [ ] each viewport has one clear primary reading anchor
- [ ] primary, secondary, supporting, and control content have different visual weights
- [ ] layouts vary by information relationship rather than arbitrary grid variety
- [ ] asymmetry, alignment, and empty space clarify content rather than decorate it
- [ ] dense sections remain dense where scanning benefits from it

## Spacing and surfaces

- [ ] section, component, and inline spacing use different rhythms
- [ ] grouping is understandable without wrapping every fact in a card
- [ ] borders and surfaces carry meaning and remain restrained
- [ ] nested cards, glass, glow, decorative gradients, and excessive shadow are absent
- [ ] radii and pills are limited to controls or a justified brand language

## Technical components

- [ ] tables and record registers have clear headers, row rhythm, alignment, states, and overflow behavior
- [ ] navigation is compact, quiet, keyboard-visible, and stateful
- [ ] dialogs or drawers have intentional width, hierarchy, close behavior, metadata grouping, and long-content handling
- [ ] diagrams and charts share the artifact's type, spacing, border, and color hierarchy
- [ ] evidence links, code, paths, and traceability remain first-class rather than decorative

## Presentation rhythm

- [ ] statement, evidence, architecture, comparison, transition, and closing sections do not look identical
- [ ] large claims are followed by proportionate proof
- [ ] technical slides do not default to headline plus three equal cards
- [ ] chapter transitions use reduction or contrast without gratuitous spectacle
- [ ] the closing is visually decisive and the call to action remains explicit

## Interaction and motion

- [ ] hover, focus, selected, copied, expanded, and dialog states are finished
- [ ] motion communicates state or relationship and remains brief
- [ ] reduced-motion mode preserves all meaning and usability
- [ ] controls do not reset unrelated state

## Responsive and accessibility inputs

- [ ] desktop and laptop compositions are deliberate
- [ ] mobile is recomposed rather than merely scaled
- [ ] long labels, German text, code, tables, and diagrams do not clip
- [ ] zoom and narrow reflow preserve reading and control order
- [ ] contrast, focus visibility, target size, landmarks, names, and roles remain credible inputs to independent review

## Rendered correction loop

- [ ] representative before evidence exists
- [ ] representative after screenshots cover required routes, sections, locales, states, and viewports
- [ ] typography, composition, whitespace, hierarchy, density, cohesion, restraint, interaction, and responsiveness were assessed
- [ ] at least one bounded correction batch followed rendered inspection when a dedicated polish pass was requested
- [ ] affected states were re-rendered and retested
